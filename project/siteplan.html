Lagos Food Guide Site Plan

Site Name

Lagos Food Guide

A website dedicated to helping residents and visitors discover popular Nigerian foods and restaurants across Lagos.

Site Purpose

  • Showcase popular Lagos foods
  • Recommend restaurants
  • Provide food descriptions
  • Allow visitors to submit recommendations
  • Help tourists discover local dishes

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular Nigerian foods I should try when visiting Lagos?
  • Where can I find the best restaurant for local dishes in Lagos?
  • Which foods are spicy and which are beginner-friendly?
  • How can I recommend my favorite restaurant?

Color Schema

#F1BC00 - Buttons, highlights, links, navigation hover

#1F2937 - Header, footer, headings

#F8F9FA - Backgrounds and cards

Typography

Poppins - Headings and navigation

Open Sans - Paragraphs, forms, content
